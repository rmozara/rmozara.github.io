Affine Branching and Modular Properties of the PSU(1,1|2)/SU(1|2) WZW Coset Model

This paper studies the character decomposition and modular properties of the non-compact PSU(1,1|2)/SU(1|2) WZW coset model. The work extends calculations begun in my diploma thesis to representations with discrete and principal continuous infinite-dimensional ground states, as well as to finite-ground-state atypical representations.

The modular analysis keeps the spectral-flow structure explicit and treats typical and atypical characters within a common framework. The modular transformations of the coset branching functions are then obtained from compatibility between the numerator and denominator character decompositions.

The results provide character and modular data for the coset, rather than a construction of the complete interacting modular-invariant bulk theory.
